# Compliance flags

Places where SocialSpark itself breaks or risks a rule, found by the content engine while researching guides. Newest first. Status: open, fixed or accepted. These are for the user to decide on; they aren't legal advice, so check anything serious with a compliance contact.

| Date | Status | Rule (source) | Where SocialSpark falls short | Seriousness | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-30 | Open | "If consent has been given to share another brokerage's listing, any post featuring that listing" must "give credit to and include the name and contact information of the originating brokerage" ([RECO Bulletin 5.3](https://www.reco.on.ca/agents-and-brokerages/reco-bulletins/reco-bulletin-5-3-advertising-online/)) | The sign-off adds "Listing courtesy of <brokerage>" with no contact information (`src/lib/generate.ts`, the footer in `src/components/SlideCanvas.tsx`) | High: posts for another brokerage's listing can fall short by default | Import the listing brokerage's phone or website and add it after the name, or ask for it when the listing isn't the user's |
| 2026-10-30 | Open | "Using images, logos, and verbatim property descriptions created by someone else requires the written consent of the owner"; showing another agent's listing needs "permission from the seller or tenant through the agent representing them" (Bulletin 5.3, which also calls scraping unethical) | Any listing link can be imported, including another brokerage's, with its photos. Nothing asks whether the user has permission | High: this is the core flow. Fine for the agent's own listing, a risk for anyone else's | When the listing brokerage differs from the user's, ask them to confirm they have permission before creating a post. Get advice on the import itself |
| 2026-10-30 | Open | "Photos and video content, such as virtual showings, cannot remain online past the end date identified in the written consent"; brokerages and agents are expected to review their accounts and take down material they no longer have the right to use (Bulletin 5.3) | The Sold consent checkboxes mention dates, but SocialSpark doesn't record the end date or remind the user (`src/components/SaleConsentFields.tsx`) | Medium: the duty is the agent's, but we could make it easy to meet | Add an optional end date to the consent and send a reminder before it passes |
| 2026-10-30 | Partly fixed | Bulletin 5.1's checklist: "Does the descriptor "brokerage" or "real estate brokerage" appear with the brokerage name?" ([RECO Bulletin 5.1](https://www.reco.on.ca/agents-and-brokerages/reco-bulletins/reco-bulletin-5-1-advertising-requirements/)) | Samples fixed in the caption prompt and guides. Sign-offs still use whatever the user saved, so a profile without "Brokerage" produces a post without it. The demo posts on the site also show "Northline Realty" and "Lakeview Realty" without it (`src/components/hero/demo-posts.ts`) | Medium | Add a hint under the brokerage field in Settings: "As registered with RECO, including 'Brokerage'". Settings is being changed by other work, so this waits for it |
