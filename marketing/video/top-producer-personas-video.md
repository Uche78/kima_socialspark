# Video script: one shoot, six cuts

For the `socialspark-ad-video` skill. Written 2026-10-23 for personas 3, 5, 7, 10, 13 and 17 (`marketing/landing-pages/top-producer-personas.md`).

**Structure.** One shared demo body, recorded once. Each landing page gets its own cut, made from three swapped pieces: the opening hook, a single persona insert, and the end card. Persona 17 (mortgage brokers) shows a different product, so it gets its own full script. Persona 7 gets a French cut with French voice-over throughout, plus an English cut.

**Length:** about 38 seconds per cut (hook 4 s + body 22 s + insert 6 s + end card 6 s).

**Placeholders:**
- `⟦TIME⟧`: the measured time from listing link to finished post. Until it's measured, use "under a minute" / « moins d'une minute ».
- `⟦PROOF⟧`: an optional 4-second customer quote card, added only with written permission.

**Left out on purpose.** A video stays on a page for months, so it carries nothing that dates quickly:
- the Q1 2026 condo statistic
- the 64% Gatineau figure (2018)
- the $800 VA figure (single case study, unclear currency)
- the "5–8 listings" estimate

**Production rules:**
- Real screen recordings of the app only.
- Burned-in captions on every line, so the video works muted.
- Show finished, attractive posts. No stressed-agent stock footage.
- No rates or payment amounts readable on any mortgage sheet.

## Using the cuts as ads

The same cuts work as Meta ads for each persona, so the video ad and its landing page carry the same hook. Formats:
- 9:16 for Reels and Stories
- 4:5 for feed
- 16:9 for the landing page

Test the hooks first: run two hook variants over the same body and decide on cost per first generated post.
