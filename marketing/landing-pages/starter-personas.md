# Landing page copy: Starter personas 2, 8, 11 and 12

Listicle copy for the `socialspark-landing-pages` skill, one page per persona, in the `ListiclePage` shape from `src/lib/persona-pages.ts`. Written 2026-10-23.

## Rules for all pages

- **`⟦TIME⟧`** is the measured time from listing link to finished post, still pending. Until it's measured, use "under a minute", the site's current claim.
- **Proof:** testimonials, stats and founder stay empty, so the sections stay hidden until real proof arrives with permission. The example posts still show.
- **Plans** (`PERSONA_PLAN`, already in code): #2 Starter monthly · #8 Starter monthly · #11 Starter yearly · #12 Starter yearly.
- **Starter facts** (`src/lib/plans.ts`): $14.99 a month, or $149.90 a year (two months free, $12.49 a month), plus tax. 10 posts and 5 new versions a month. Free plan: 3 posts with an account, no credit card (1 as a guest).
- **Plan maths:** Coming Soon, Just Listed, Open House, Price Reduced and Sold on two platforms is 10 posts, so Starter covers one listing from start to finish.
- **Sourced figure used:** 61% of cottage-country agents report longer days on market (Royal LePage 2026 recreational property report, personas.md [17]).
- **Sourced figure left out:** "about a third of agents close nothing in a year" [2]. The data is several years old and it's a fear message. The rookie page sells the reward instead: a first listing that looks established.
- **Tone for #12:** never mention age, grandchildren or "keeping up". The page sells better marketing and control, not catching up.
- **Lengths:** all pages are checked against the page tests (title ≤ 46 characters, description 70–160, headline ≤ 80, hero subheading ≤ 45 words, 7–10 reasons, 4–8 FAQs).

## Gaps and checks for the landing-pages skill

1. **Persona 12, reason 1 (listing presentations):** making posts for recent listings to show sellers is fine. Posting old listings again needs the photographer's permission [15]. The FAQ on photos isn't on this page, so add it if the page gets a photo-reuse question.
2. **Persona 11, reason 7, and persona 12, FAQ 5:** "Move up to Pro for those months" and "your assistant can set it up" assume plan changes mid-year and shared login are acceptable. Confirm that changing plans mid-year works in billing, and that you're comfortable with an assistant using the agent's login.
3. **"Draft only you can see" (persona 12, reason 3):** confirm posts aren't visible to anyone else before publishing. That's true today with no team accounts, but recheck it when Team ships.
4. **Persona 8's comparison row "Usually yearly"** describes design tools in general. Drop the row if you'd rather not characterise other products' billing at all.
