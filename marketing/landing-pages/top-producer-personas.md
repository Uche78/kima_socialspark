# Landing page copy: personas 5, 7 and 13

Listicle copy for the `socialspark-landing-pages` skill, one page per persona, in the `ListiclePage` shape from `src/lib/persona-pages.ts`. Written 2026-10-23. It matches the angles in `marketing/ads/top-producer-personas.md`, so each ad lands on a page making the same promise.

## Rules for all pages

- **`⟦TIME⟧`** is the measured time from listing link to finished post, still pending. Until it's measured, use "under a minute" (EN) or « moins d'une minute » (FR), the site's current claim.
- **Proof:** testimonials, stats and founder stay empty, so the sections stay hidden until real proof arrives with permission. The example posts still show.
- **Plans** (`PERSONA_PLAN`, already in code): #5 Top Producer monthly · #7 Top Producer yearly · #13 Top Producer yearly.
- **Plan maths:** every post type on two platforms is at most 12 posts a listing, so 50 posts covers at least 4 listings in full.
- **Sourced figure:** $400 a month for about 40 posts is one Canadian brokerage's social content plan (personas.md [9]). Pages say "one Canadian brokerage's", never "the typical cost".
- **SEO, AEO and GEO:**
  - The title tag carries the search phrase, and the eyebrow repeats it near the H1.
  - Every FAQ answer opens with a direct, self-contained answer and names SocialSpark where an AI might quote it alone.
  - Figures are exact and sourced, and the comparison table makes clean, quotable rows.
- **Lengths checked against the page tests:** title ≤ 46 characters, description 70–160, headline ≤ 80, hero subheading ≤ 45 words, 7–10 reasons, 4–8 FAQs.

   - `lang="fr-CA"` on the page
   - French versions of the shared UI strings around this copy
   - hreflang links between 2a and 2b
   
   If the app interface stays English, say so honestly on the French page, for example in a FAQ: « L'application est en anglais; vos publications sont en français. » Confirm what's true before publishing.
2. **French post-type names.** Reason 5 on page 2a names the six types in French. Check that the on-image badge actually renders in French when the language is French. If it doesn't, describe the types without implying the badge is translated.
3. **Brand & voice is an English UI label.** It's kept as-is on the French page because that's what the user will see in the app.
4. **Persona 5's FAQ 5** says team seats are planned, not available. Keep that wording until Team ships.
5. **Persona 13's headline** names the price. If prices change, update the headline with `plans.ts`.
