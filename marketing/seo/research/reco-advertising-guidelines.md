# Gap brief: reco advertising guidelines

Keyword: "reco advertising guidelines" — ~110/mo average in Canada (Keyword Planner, pulled 2026-10-30; range 70–170/mo over the trailing 12 months). "reco advertising rules" adds ~10/mo. Topic chosen by the user for today's run instead of the top-scored opportunity, for Ontario realtors. No existing SocialSpark guide covers this as its main subject: `just-listed-post-ideas.ts`, `real-estate-captions-for-instagram.ts` and `real-estate-facebook-post-ideas.ts` each cite RECO Bulletins 5.1 and 5.4 in one list item inside a "rules" section, not as a full treatment — not cannibalisation.

## Pages read

1. **https://closeflow.ca/blog/reco-advertising-rules-ontario** — "RECO Advertising Rules for Ontario Realtors (TRESA Guide)". Covers brokerage ID, permitted-terms table, social media bullet points, sold-property consent table, team rules, and cites discipline penalties (without a RECO citation).
2. **https://consultusdigital.com/blog/digital-advertising-rules-for-realtors-in-canada/** — "Digital Advertising Rules for Realtors in Canada". Covers RECO/CREA basics plus platform ad-policy overlays (Meta Special Ad Category, Google Ads, TikTok disclosure).
3. **https://social.agentlocator.ca/reco-guidelines/** — "RECO Guidelines". Short page on social media conduct (disclosure, accuracy, professionalism); doesn't mention brokerage name requirements at all.
4. **https://www.orea.com/resources/media-room/special-bulletin/2015/June/Avoid-advertising-pitfalls** — OREA's 2015 "Avoid advertising pitfalls" bulletin. Oldest page found; predates TRESA (Dec 1, 2023) entirely.

(A fifth result, elliottlawyers.com's "Summary: Advertising Guidelines for Real Estate Agents in Ontario", returned an "Account Suspended" page and was skipped.)

## Table stakes all four cover

- Brokerage name must appear, clearly, in all advertising.
- Use your RECO-registered name, not a nickname.
- Claims must be truthful, accurate, verifiable.
- The same rules apply online and on social media as anywhere else.
- A sold property needs consent before you can advertise it as sold.

## Missing opportunities (what none of the four give a reader, and where this guide puts it)

1. **No page has the full permitted-terms table by registration type**, including Broker of Record's exact requirement and PRECs being barred from advertising at all. Pages mention PREC restrictions only in passing (closeflow, one sentence) or not at all. → Section "The exact words you're allowed to use", a table from RECO Bulletin 5.2, with a PREC callout.
2. **No page states the sold-property consent rule as a clean before/after × price/no-price table.** Closeflow comes closest but the matrix in Bulletin 5.4 (which also covers buyer's vs. seller's brokerage) is more complete. → Section "Advertising a sold listing", a table built from Bulletin 5.4 directly.
3. **Testimonials are a named gap on every page that mentions them** (closeflow: "the article notes testimonials have separate consent rules but provides zero guidance"; the others don't mention testimonials at all). RECO has no bulletin titled "testimonials", but Bulletin 5.1's accuracy/verifiability rule and Bulletin 5.3's consent-for-photos-and-names rule both apply directly to a client quote or a client's photo in a review post. → FAQ entry spelling this out, since it's a real question with no clear answer on the web and a direct answer from the two bulletins that do apply.
4. **Social media specifics are thin everywhere** ("brokerage identity must be readily visible", no description of where that actually has to go). Bulletin 5.3 itself says "readily visible or accessible on the account profile" — still not concrete, but it does add team-website rules, third-party-managed-site responsibility, and a consent-duration rule for photos/video (must come down by the consent's expiry date) that none of the four pages mention. → Section "The rules for social media specifically", with the profile/bio placement and the takedown-by-expiry-date rule, which is a genuinely new fact to a reader of the other four pages.
5. **No page gives sourced, current discipline penalties.** Closeflow states "$50,000 (agents) or $100,000 (brokerages)" with no citation. This guide verifies the figures against RECO's own registrant guide to discipline and appeal hearings (for offences after December 1, 2023) and cites it, plus the non-financial penalties (education, conditions, suspension, revocation) that the blog posts omit. → Section "What happens if you break the rules", sourced directly, and the dollar figures go in `figures` per the copy-rules check.
6. **The OREA page is nine years out of date** (June 2015) and still ranks; nothing currently on the first results page reflects TRESA, which took effect December 1, 2023 and changed the discipline committee's jurisdiction and added the "clearly and prominently" and "readily visible or accessible" language this guide quotes. → The guide states the TRESA effective date up front and treats everything as current law, which the top results mostly don't.
7. **Team advertising gets one line on closeflow** ("no special treatment") and nothing on the others. → Section folds this into the permitted-terms section: every member named, in their own registered term, brokerage still required.
8. **None of the four pages shows what a compliant social caption's sign-off actually looks like**, despite this being the exact sentence-level thing a realtor opens the page to copy. → `example` block with a compliant sign-off, consistent with the sign-off format already used in sibling guides ("Jane Doe, REALTOR® | ABC Realty Inc.").
