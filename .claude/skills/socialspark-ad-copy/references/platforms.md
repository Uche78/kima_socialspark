# Platform formats

Limits change. If a limit matters and you have web access, confirm it against the platform's current help pages.

## Google Ads: Responsive Search Ads (RSA)

| Asset | Count | Max chars |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | 3–15 (write all 15) | 30 |
| Description | 2–4 (write all 4) | 90 |
| Display path | 2 | 15 each |
| Sitelink text / descriptions | 4+ | 25 / 35 each line |
| Callout | 4+ | 25 |
| Structured snippet value | 3+ per header | 25 |

Rules:
- Google mixes headlines in any order, so **each one must stand alone** and none may repeat another's idea. Spread across: keyword match (2–3), benefit (4–5), specifics/proof (3–4), offer/risk reversal (2–3), call to action (1–2).
- Put the searcher's keyword in at least 2–3 headlines (e.g. "Real Estate Social Media Posts").
- Pin only when compliance requires it; pinning lowers ad strength.
- No exclamation marks in headlines; at most one per description. No ALL CAPS words, no gimmicky punctuation.
- Search intent is already there, so lead with the match and the demonstration ("Paste a Link, Get a Post").
- Suggest keywords in tight themes (listing post generator, real estate instagram posts, just listed post template, mortgage feature sheet, open house social post) and negatives (jobs, course, free template download if not converting, realtor.ca login, etc.).

## Meta: Facebook and Instagram feed, Stories, Reels

| Field | Recommended | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | First 125 chars must work alone | Truncated with "See more" around 125 chars on most placements |
| Headline | ≤ 40 chars (27 shows reliably) | Below the image on Facebook; hidden or small on many IG placements |
| Description | ≤ 25–30 chars | Often not shown; never put essential info here |
| CTA button | Pick from Meta's list | Sign Up, Learn More, Get Started, Try It (availability varies) |

Rules:
- **Hook in the first line.** Lead with the reader and the result, or a scene they recognise ("Just listed at 4 p.m. Posted by 4:01.").
- On Instagram the image or video carries the ad; copy supports it. Captions can't hold clickable links; the CTA button does that.
- Stories/Reels: text overlay ≤ ~8 words, inside safe zones (keep the top and bottom ~14% clear). Provide an overlay line separately from the primary text.
- Give 2–3 primary-text lengths per angle: short (≤ 125), medium (2–4 short lines), long (problem → demonstration → risk reversal → CTA). Long copy often wins for considered B2B purchases; test it, don't assume.
- **Personal attributes policy:** don't assert or imply the reader's personal characteristics (financial status, health, etc.). Addressing a profession ("Realtors:") is fine; "Struggling to pay your bills?" is not.
- **Special Ad Category: Housing.** Meta may classify ads showing homes for sale, prices or mortgage payments as housing ads, which restricts targeting. Note this in the delivery when creative shows listings or payment sheets, and frame the ad clearly as software for agents and brokers.

## Canada-specific

- Competition Act: claims must be true and not misleading in their general impression; free offers must state material conditions ("3 free posts, no credit card").
- Prices in CAD and say "plus tax" where a price appears.
- Québec: commercial advertising aimed at Québec should be in French (Charter of the French Language). Write Québec French natively; English-only creative for Québec placements is a risk.
- Mortgage broker audiences: never imply SocialSpark sets, suggests or guarantees rates or approvals.
